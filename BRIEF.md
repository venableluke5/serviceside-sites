# ServiceSide Sites Website Brief

## Business

ServiceSide Sites

## Tagline

Simple websites for local service businesses.

## Primary audience

Small local service businesses, including:

- HVAC companies
- Contractors
- Landscapers
- Cleaners
- Handymen
- Barbers
- Small gyms
- Tutors
- Mobile detailers

## Primary website goal

Convince a local business owner that ServiceSide Sites is trustworthy, practical, affordable, and easy to work with.

The primary conversion is requesting a free website mockup.

## Main offer

I build simple, professional websites for local service businesses so customers can quickly understand what the business does, trust it, and contact it.

## Current starter services

- One-page websites
- Three-page websites
- Mobile-friendly design
- Homepage copy cleanup
- Service sections
- Service-area wording
- Contact forms
- Click-to-call buttons
- Basic on-page SEO
- Google Business Profile cleanup guidance
- Monthly website maintenance

## Brand personality

- Clear
- Practical
- Professional
- Approachable
- Local
- Honest
- Not flashy
- Not corporate
- Not like a large agency

## Visual direction

- Clean and professional
- Strong typography
- Spacious but not empty
- Modern without looking like a tech startup
- Realistic and trustworthy
- Appropriate for contractors and local service businesses
- Use the existing ServiceSide Sites color palette
- Avoid excessive cards, pills, gradients, animations, and decorative effects

## Current brand colors

- Primary: #20343A
- Accent: #2F7E89
- Background: #F7FAFA
- Surface: #FFFFFF
- Muted text: #667579
- Border: #DDE6E7

## Important restrictions

- Do not invent testimonials.
- Do not invent client results.
- Do not guarantee rankings.
- Do not claim years of experience that have not been provided.
- Do not describe ServiceSide Sites as a large agency.
- Do not replace approved branding without a clear reason.
- Do not redesign the entire website when asked for refinements.
- Do not remove working content without explaining why.

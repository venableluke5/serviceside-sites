# Natural Outdoor

## Best industries for this style

- Landscaping
- Lawn care
- Tree service
- Hardscaping
- Irrigation
- Outdoor lighting
- Fence and patio companies

## Overall mood

Fresh, grounded, outdoors-focused, and clean. This style should feel connected to real property improvement, not eco-lifestyle branding.

## Recommended color direction

- Use natural greens, warm stone tones, and off-whites.
- Good directions: moss and cream, deep green with clay, sage with charcoal.
- Accent colors should still support strong calls to action.

## Typography direction

- Use a refined but practical serif or humanist sans for headings.
- Pair with a plain readable sans-serif for body copy.
- Headings can feel slightly more open and calm than heavy trades styles.

## Hero layout

- Wide image-led hero or split hero with strong outdoor imagery.
- Keep text block clear and grounded with a simple CTA group.
- A short service-area or seasonal-services note works well near the hero.

## Section order

1. Hero
2. Services
3. Project/gallery section
4. Why choose us
5. Service area
6. Reviews
7. Process
8. FAQ
9. Contact form

## Image treatment

- Use real property photos, yard transformations, crews, and finished outdoor spaces.
- Natural light works best.
- Avoid dreamy filters, washed-out edits, or generic nature stock.

## Card style

- Use soft but structured cards with balanced spacing.
- A mix of image cards and text cards works well.
- Subtle background changes between sections help this style feel intentional.

## Button style

- Filled buttons with earthy contrast.
- Secondary buttons can be outlined or sand-toned.
- Keep wording practical: `Request Estimate`, `View Services`, `Ask About Your Property`.

## Animation style

- Soft reveal animations and light hover shifts are enough.
- Motion can be a touch calmer than bold trade styles.
- Keep all motion secondary to clarity.

## What to avoid

- Boho or boutique garden-center styling
- Overly delicate scripts
- Too many leaf or nature-themed decorative graphics
- Lifestyle blog layouts
- Weak CTA contrast

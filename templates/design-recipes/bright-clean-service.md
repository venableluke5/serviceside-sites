# Bright Clean Service

## Best industries for this style

- House cleaning
- Commercial cleaning
- Window cleaning
- Pressure washing
- Junk removal
- Organizing services
- Mobile detailing

## Overall mood

Fresh, efficient, clear, and upbeat. This should feel clean and easy to trust, not sterile or generic.

## Recommended color direction

- Use light backgrounds with fresh accent colors.
- Good directions: white with sky blue, pale gray with teal, soft navy with bright aqua.
- Use contrast carefully so the site stays readable and does not look washed out.

## Typography direction

- Use crisp sans-serif headings and body text.
- Headings can be bold but should stay open and readable.
- Avoid rugged or overly condensed fonts.

## Hero layout

- Clean stacked or split hero with a simple service promise and CTA.
- A before-and-after image, team image, or bright service photo can work well.
- Add quick trust points directly under the primary CTA.

## Section order

1. Hero
2. Services
3. Why choose us
4. Reviews
5. Process
6. Service area
7. FAQ
8. Contact form
9. Trust bar

## Image treatment

- Use bright, well-lit, real photos with clean composition.
- Focus on visible results, organized spaces, or crews at work.
- Avoid overly staged stock imagery with exaggerated smiles.

## Card style

- Use light cards with crisp borders or gentle shadows.
- Spacing should feel airy but not sparse.
- Icons can work well if kept simple.

## Button style

- Rounded or softly rounded buttons with strong color contrast.
- Filled main CTA and simple secondary button.
- Button labels should stay service-focused and easy to understand.

## Animation style

- Short, clean, almost invisible motion.
- Quick fade, slight rise, light hover state.
- Avoid dramatic parallax or layered motion.

## What to avoid

- Pure white everywhere with no structure
- Medical-clinic sterility
- Oversized bubbly shapes that feel childish
- Too many blue gradients that feel generic SaaS
- Fake sparkle or shine effects

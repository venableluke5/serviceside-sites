# Bold Trades

## Best industries for this style

- HVAC
- Roofing
- Electrical
- Garage doors
- Pest control
- Plumbing companies that want a stronger sales-first look

## Overall mood

Direct, confident, fast-moving, and built to feel action-oriented without looking gimmicky.

## Recommended color direction

- Use a dark base with one strong high-contrast accent.
- Good directions: charcoal with safety orange, navy with bright red, deep slate with electric blue.
- Keep backgrounds simple so buttons and service highlights stand out.

## Typography direction

- Use a bold condensed or sturdy sans-serif for headings.
- Use a clean readable sans-serif for body copy.
- Headings should feel strong and compact, not elegant or delicate.

## Hero layout

- Left-aligned headline and short service-driven subhead.
- Strong primary CTA near the top: call, request estimate, or book service.
- Right side can use a jobsite photo, tech/team image, or strong contact panel.

## Section order

1. Hero
2. Trust bar
3. Services
4. Why choose us
5. Reviews
6. Service area
7. Process
8. Contact form
9. FAQ

## Image treatment

- Use high-contrast real photos with clear subject focus.
- Show crews, trucks, equipment, or in-progress work when available.
- Avoid soft lifestyle imagery or vague abstract stock photos.

## Card style

- Use solid cards with strong borders or shadow separation.
- Keep corners moderate, not overly rounded.
- Use icon or label support, but do not over-decorate.

## Button style

- Large, high-contrast, easy to tap.
- Prefer filled buttons over outline buttons for the main CTA.
- Button text should be practical: `Call Now`, `Request Estimate`, `Schedule Service`.

## Animation style

- Keep motion quick and minimal.
- Use simple fade or short upward reveal on scroll.
- Avoid slow luxury animations or floating effects.

## What to avoid

- Thin elegant typography
- Pastel-heavy palettes
- Too much white space with weak hierarchy
- Fancy editorial layouts
- Startup-style abstract graphics

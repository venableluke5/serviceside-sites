# Rugged Contractor

## Best industries for this style

- General contractors
- Excavation
- Concrete
- Roofing
- Siding
- Deck builders
- Heavy outdoor trade businesses

## Overall mood

Grounded, durable, workmanlike, and no-nonsense. It should feel built for serious projects and solid field work.

## Recommended color direction

- Use earthy dark neutrals with one strong accent.
- Good directions: iron gray with rust, olive with black, weathered navy with tan.
- Backgrounds can feel slightly textured through color variation, but not through heavy graphic effects.

## Typography direction

- Use broad, sturdy sans-serif headings with weight and presence.
- Body type should stay simple and compact.
- Avoid refined editorial serif pairings unless they are heavily restrained.

## Hero layout

- Strong headline over a wide project photo or split layout with bold service copy.
- Include an immediate action row with call button and estimate request.
- Good place for a compact “project types” or “coverage area” panel.

## Section order

1. Hero
2. Services
3. Project/gallery section
4. Why choose us
5. Process
6. Reviews
7. Service area
8. FAQ
9. Contact form

## Image treatment

- Use real project photos with texture, materials, machinery, or finished work.
- Slightly moodier or higher-contrast images can work well.
- Avoid polished corporate office photography.

## Card style

- Use thicker borders, grounded shadows, and denser spacing.
- Cards can feel more blocky and structured.
- Labeling and small metadata work well here.

## Button style

- Strong rectangular or lightly rounded buttons.
- Filled buttons should feel sturdy, not glossy.
- Use direct labels like `Request Quote`, `Call Today`, `Start Your Project`.

## Animation style

- Minimal and restrained.
- Subtle section reveals are enough.
- Do not use floaty animations, oversized hover effects, or decorative motion.

## What to avoid

- Soft spa-like styling
- Excessively round shapes everywhere
- Polished SaaS dashboards or “modern app” UI
- Too much copy above the fold
- Decorative illustration-heavy sections

# Premium Local Service

## Best industries for this style

- High-end painting
- Custom remodeling
- Luxury cleaning or home management
- Fine landscaping
- Pool service
- Specialty home maintenance
- Established local brands serving higher-value properties

## Overall mood

Refined, calm, polished, and high-trust. This should feel premium because of restraint and clarity, not because it copies luxury fashion branding.

## Recommended color direction

- Use restrained neutrals with one rich accent.
- Good directions: warm ivory with deep charcoal, slate with muted brass, taupe with forest green.
- Contrast should stay practical enough for everyday local-business use.

## Typography direction

- Use an elegant but readable serif or polished sans-serif for headings.
- Pair with a clean understated sans-serif for body text.
- Keep typography spacious and controlled, not ornate.

## Hero layout

- Wide breathing room with a clean headline, selective supporting copy, and a focused CTA.
- Strong fit for split hero with image on one side and trust/contact panel on the other.
- Optional small stat or service-quality strip can work if facts are real.

## Section order

1. Hero
2. Why choose us
3. Services
4. Project/gallery section
5. Reviews
6. Process
7. Service area
8. FAQ
9. Contact form

## Image treatment

- Use high-quality real photos with thoughtful framing and consistent editing.
- Finished-work photography matters more than action-heavy jobsite shots here.
- Avoid stock luxury interiors unless the client truly serves that market and has no better assets.

## Card style

- Use quieter cards with careful spacing, soft shadows, and restrained borders.
- Cards should feel deliberate and polished, not busy.
- Fewer stronger cards usually work better than many small ones.

## Button style

- Buttons should feel clear and confident, not flashy.
- Filled primary button with a restrained secondary button works best.
- Avoid giant aggressive CTA styling unless the client’s sales style demands it.

## Animation style

- Motion should be subtle, smooth, and limited.
- Section reveals, soft image fades, and controlled hover states are enough.
- Keep attention on content quality and photography.

## What to avoid

- Black-and-gold “fake luxury” treatment
- Tiny low-contrast text
- Over-minimal pages with too little service information
- Fashion-brand typography choices
- Aggressive trade styling that fights the premium positioning

# Clean Family-Owned

## Best industries for this style

- Plumbing
- HVAC
- Appliance repair
- Carpet cleaning
- Local movers
- General home services with a relationship-first reputation

## Overall mood

Friendly, trustworthy, steady, and approachable. This should feel personal and local without leaning sentimental.

## Recommended color direction

- Use soft neutrals with one dependable accent color.
- Good directions: navy and cream, forest green and warm white, muted blue and sand.
- Keep contrast strong enough for readability and mobile clarity.

## Typography direction

- Use a friendly serif or soft-edged sans-serif for headings.
- Use a clean, highly readable sans-serif for body text.
- Avoid fonts that feel corporate-tech or luxury-brand.

## Hero layout

- Simple two-column or stacked hero with headline, short intro, and clear contact action.
- Include a team, truck, homeowner, or real service image when available.
- Add a small trust strip or quick contact block beside the intro.

## Section order

1. Hero
2. Trust bar
3. Why choose us
4. Services
5. Reviews
6. Process
7. Service area
8. FAQ
9. Contact form

## Image treatment

- Use bright natural photos with real people when possible.
- Prioritize approachable scenes over dramatic action shots.
- Keep editing light and realistic.

## Card style

- Use soft cards with light borders, warm backgrounds, and comfortable spacing.
- Rounded corners can be slightly more generous than rugged styles.
- Keep the layout calm and easy to scan.

## Button style

- Rounded, friendly, and obvious.
- Use a filled main button and a lighter secondary option.
- Avoid aggressive all-caps button styling unless the brand already uses it.

## Animation style

- Very light reveal animations only.
- Prioritize calm polish over motion.
- Good fit: fade-in, short stagger, subtle hover lift.

## What to avoid

- Harsh industrial color schemes unless the brand already uses them
- Overly macho or aggressive copy styling
- Luxury black-and-gold treatment
- Ultra-minimal layouts with too little context
- Cheesy “small-town family values” visuals

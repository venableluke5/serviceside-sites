| Role | Color |
| --- | --- |
| Primary | `#20343A` |

| Background | `#F7FAFA` |
| Surface | `#FFFFFF` |
| Muted text | `#667579` |

## Direction

- Keep the first mockup simple and credible.
- Prioritize clear service, location, phone number, and request action.
- Use real client photos when available.
- Avoid stock-heavy layouts unless the client has no usable imagery.
- Keep sections easy to scan for local service customers.

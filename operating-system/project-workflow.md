# Project Workflow

## 1. Lead

Find a local service business that has:

- No website
- Facebook-only presence
- Outdated website
- Weak contact information
- Poor mobile layout
- Unclear services

## 2. Preview

Build a simple homepage preview.

Rules:

- Do not overbuild.
- Do not create a full site for free.
- Do not publish fake pages under their name.
- Keep it to a homepage concept.

## 3. Pitch

Send a soft message.

Goal:
Start a conversation, not pressure them.

## 4. Intake

If interested, send the intake form.

Do not start full build until:

- Package is chosen
- Deposit is paid
- Required content is collected

## 5. Build

Use the local service template.

Basic build order:

1. Homepage
2. Services section/page
3. Contact section/page
4. Mobile layout
5. SEO title/meta
6. Review before sending

## 6. Review

Before sending to client, check:

- No placeholders
- No bracketed text
- Phone buttons work
- Contact form is clear
- Mobile layout works
- Services are obvious
- Trust points are visible
- Site does not feel overbuilt

## 7. Revisions

Includes 2 rounds of revisions.

Revision rules:

- Collect all client changes in one message when possible.
- Do not accept unlimited drip-feed changes.
- Larger changes are quoted separately.

## 8. Final payment

Collect final payment before launch.

## 9. Launch

Run launch checklist.

## 10. Handoff

Send handoff email and offer monthly care.

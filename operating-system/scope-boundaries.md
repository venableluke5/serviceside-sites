# Scope Boundaries

Every package includes clear limits.

## Included

- 1 kickoff/intake form
- 1 initial draft/mockup
- 2 rounds of revisions
- Agreed number of pages
- Basic contact form
- Click-to-call button
- Basic SEO titles/headings/meta descriptions
- Mobile-friendly layout
- Launch support

## Not included

- Unlimited changes
- Ongoing copywriting beyond agreed pages
- Logo redesign unless quoted separately
- Full SEO campaigns
- Google Ads
- Social media management
- Custom software
- Ecommerce
- Complex booking systems
- Blog writing
- Customer phone calls
- Reputation management
- Emergency updates

## Monthly care boundary

Monthly care includes up to 30 minutes of small updates per month. Unused time does not roll over. Larger changes are quoted separately.

## Revision boundary

Each website includes 2 revision rounds.

A revision round means the client sends a grouped list of requested changes. Small edits are included. New pages, new layouts, major rewrites, or new features are quoted separately.

## Goal

Build a simple, trustworthy website that helps customers quickly understand what the business does, where it serves, and how to contact them.

## Included

- Agreed number of pages
- Mobile-friendly layout
- Services section/page
- Service area section
- About/trust section
- Contact form
- Click-to-call button
- Google review button if available
- Basic SEO title and meta description
- 2 revision rounds
- Launch support

## Not included

- Unlimited edits
- Full SEO campaign
- Paid ads
- Social media management
- Logo redesign unless quoted separately
- Ecommerce
- Complex booking system
- Custom software
- Ongoing updates unless on monthly care

## Payment

50% deposit before build.
50% final payment before launch.

## Monthly care

Monthly care is available starting at $59/month.

Includes up to 30 minutes of small updates per month. Unused time does not roll over. Larger changes are quoted separately.

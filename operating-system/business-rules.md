# Business Rules

## What ServiceSide Sites does

I build simple, professional websites for local service businesses so customers can quickly understand what they do, trust them, and contact them.

## Best-fit clients

- HVAC
- Plumbing
- Electrical
- Landscaping
- Cleaning
- Handyman
- Auto detailers
- Small contractors
- Local repair/service businesses
- Businesses with only Facebook pages
- Businesses with outdated websites

## What I sell

- Clean, simple websites
- Better first impression
- Clear services
- Service area information
- Click-to-call buttons
- Contact forms
- Basic on-page SEO structure
- Google review links
- Simple monthly updates

## What I do not sell

- Guaranteed rankings
- Full SEO campaigns
- Paid ads
- Social media management
- Complex booking systems
- Ecommerce
- Custom web apps
- Unlimited edits
- Reputation management drama
- Constant emergency updates

## Main promise

A simple, trustworthy website that makes it easier for local customers to understand, trust, and contact the business.

# Packages and Pricing

## Free Preview

Price: Free

Best for:

- Warm leads
- Local prospects
- Businesses with outdated sites
- Businesses with only Facebook pages

Includes:

- Homepage concept only
- Basic layout idea
- No public/live website
- No revisions unless they become a client

Purpose:
Start a conversation and show what the business could look like online.

# Big10 Cheer Project

## Business

Business name: Big10 Cheer
Current website: https://www.big10cheer.com
Project type: Prospect redesign mockup
Stage: Research and design direction

## Brand Naming

Project folder/domain reference: Big10 Cheer / big10cheer.com

Public-facing brand used in the redesign:
Cheer Magic All Stars

Use the supplied Cheer Magic All Stars branding in the mockup.
Do not present Big10 Cheer as the main customer-facing name unless later
information confirms that it should be used publicly.

## Mockup Goal

Create a modern, energetic, professional cheer and tumbling website that
appeals to parents and athletes.

The design should feel custom and significantly more polished than a basic
AI-generated website.

The mockup should make programs easy to understand and make registration or
contacting the gym obvious.

## Target Audience

- Parents looking for cheer or tumbling programs
- Children and teenagers interested in competitive cheer
- Current families looking for schedules or program information

## Primary Actions

- View programs
- Contact the gym
- Register or request information

## Available Assets

- Business logo is in the assets folder
- Website images are in the assets or source folder
- Current website screenshots are in the screenshots folder

## Current Website Problems

- Information needs stronger organization
- The design feels dated
- Important programs and actions are not visually prominent
- The website needs stronger mobile presentation
- The site needs more energy and visual personality

## Proposed Design Direction

Create a bold, photo-driven athletic design that still feels trustworthy and
clear to parents.

Use strong typography, dynamic photo crops, layered section compositions,
subtle movement, and clear program navigation.

Avoid making it feel like a generic corporate website or a collection of
ordinary rounded cards.

## Important Rules

- Do not invent programs, prices, schedules, credentials, or testimonials
- Clearly identify information that cannot be verified
- Use real Big10 assets wherever possible
- Do not copy the Slater Heating layout
- Do not begin coding until the design direction has been reviewed
